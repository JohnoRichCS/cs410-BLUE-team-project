Faíct - The Fact AI Checking Tool

Evidence-first claim verification that helps users understand what can be verified, what conditions matter, and which sources support the result.

Elevator Pitch

Faíct is a browser-based claim verification tool designed to help college students evaluate questionable information using relevant and authoritative evidence.

Instead of simply returning an answer, Faíct identifies the type of claim, retrieves applicable sources, highlights important conditions or missing context, and provides a qualified result with evidence the user can review.

Problem Description

College students frequently encounter claims about financial aid, academic policies, research, and other topics through social media, search results, and conversations. These claims may leave out important rules, conditions, or exceptions.

Verifying this information manually can require searching multiple websites, deciding which sources are authoritative, and determining whether the information actually applies to the student's situation.

Customer and End User

The primary end users for the Faíct prototype are undergraduate college students. The current demonstration focuses on financial aid claims because these questions often depend on enrollment status, aid type, timing, and institutional policies.

The broader system may later support students, researchers, educators, journalists, and other users who need transparent evidence-based claim verification.

Solution Statement

  • Accept a written claim through a browser-based interface
  • Classify the claim and identify conditional or missing context
  • Retrieve and prioritize relevant authoritative sources
  • Evaluate the relevance of retrieved evidence
  • Provide a qualified verification result and explanation
  • Display source links and evidence used during verification
  • Preserve completed verification results for later review
